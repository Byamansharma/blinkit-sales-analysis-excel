# Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Blinkit's grocery sales data using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to understand overall sales performance and identify patterns across product categories, outlet types, outlet sizes, outlet locations, fat content and outlet establishment years.

The analysis was converted into an interactive Excel dashboard to present key performance indicators and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyse overall sales performance
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Compare sales across different outlet types and sizes
- Analyse sales across different outlet locations
- Understand the contribution of different fat-content segments
- Identify sales trends based on outlet establishment year
- Build a dashboard for easier business decision-making

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 1. Sales by Fat Content
Compared total sales between Low Fat and Regular products.

### 2. Sales by Item Type
Analysed sales performance across different product categories.

### 3. Fat Content by Outlet Location
Compared Low Fat and Regular product sales across Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 locations.

### 4. Sales by Outlet Establishment Year
Analysed how sales varied across different outlet establishment years.

### 5. Sales by Outlet Size
Compared sales contribution from High, Medium and Small outlets.

### 6. Sales by Outlet Location
Analysed total sales across Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 locations.

### 7. Metrics by Outlet Type
Compared total sales, average sales and number of records across different outlet types.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Low Fat products contributed approximately 65% of total sales.
- Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods were among the highest-performing item categories.
- Tier 3 outlets generated the highest sales among the three location tiers.
- Medium-sized outlets contributed the highest share of sales among outlet-size segments.
- Supermarket Type 1 generated the largest share of sales among outlet types.
- Sales peaked around the 2018 outlet establishment year in the analysed dataset.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

- Maintain strong inventory availability for high-performing product categories.
- Investigate the reasons behind weaker-performing categories.
- Analyse successful Tier 3 and Supermarket Type 1 outlets to identify practices that could be replicated.
- Review product assortment and availability across lower-performing outlet segments.
- Combine sales data with pricing, inventory, promotion and profitability data for deeper analysis.

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

The dataset provides sales-related information but does not contain sufficient information about profit margins, promotions, stock-outs or detailed customer behaviour.

Therefore, the analysis identifies sales patterns and potential areas for investigation rather than proving the exact causes behind sales performance.
